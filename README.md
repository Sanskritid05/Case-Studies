# 📚 Case Study Competition Repository

## 🌍 Overview
This repository contains **idea proposals and research notes** developed for various **case study competitions** across multiple domains such as **business strategy, technology, finance, sustainability, and public policy**.

The goal of this repository is to document **problem understanding, background research, and potential solution ideas** for different case challenges. Instead of detailed quantitative analysis or technical implementation, the focus is on **structured ideation and research-backed proposals** that outline possible strategic directions.

These cases reflect the type of challenges commonly seen in **B-school competitions, consulting case challenges, tech innovation contests, and sustainability-focused initiatives**.

---

The case studies in this repository may cover topics such as:

### 💼 Business & Strategy
- Market expansion strategies  
- Financial services frameworks  
- Business model innovation  
- Competitive positioning  

### 💻 Technology & Innovation
- Tech-enabled solutions for real-world problems

- Sustainable agriculture and supply chains

### 🏛️ Public Policy & Development
- Financial inclusion initiatives  
- Regional development strategies  
- Digital adoption in rural and underserved regions  

---

## 📂 Structure of Each Case Study

Each case study folder generally includes:

- **📌 Problem Statement** – The original challenge or competition prompt  
- **📖 Background Research** – Industry context, supporting information, and relevant trends  
- **💡 Idea Proposal** – Conceptual solution or strategic direction for the problem  
- **🔗 References** – Sources used during research
